Refund Policy

Refund terms for GROW MORE SOLUTIONS digital products and TradingView access.

Digital products

Because indicators, software and access rights are digital products, customers should review the selected product and plan before payment.

Payment issues

If a payment is debited but the order remains pending, contact support with the order ID. Access is activated only after payment verification.

Access problems

If you purchased a TradingView invite-only product and supplied the wrong username, contact support promptly so the access request can be corrected.

Refund requests

Any refund request is reviewed according to the applicable product terms, payment status and service-delivery status. Contact support with your order ID and registered email.
