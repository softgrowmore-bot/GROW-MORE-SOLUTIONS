Privacy Policy

We use the information required to create accounts, process orders and deliver product access.

Information we collect

Name, email, phone number, order details and, when required for a TradingView invite-only product, your TradingView username.

How we use it

We use this information to authenticate customers, process payments, maintain order history, manage product entitlements and grant requested access.

Payment information

Payment details are handled by the configured payment provider. We do not ask customers to send card, UPI PIN or banking passwords through chat.

Security

Customer passwords are stored using one-way password hashing. Access to private product files is protected by authenticated account checks.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details published on the website.
